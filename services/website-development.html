How we build it

From first call to launch

A clear process, so you know what happens at each step and what we need from you.

  1. Discovery

    We discuss your goals, your customers and the pages your site needs. You get a written proposal with scope, timeline and pricing.

  2. Structure and content

    We agree the page structure and what content is needed — text, images and your logo or brand assets — and who provides each part.

  3. Design

    We design the look and layout of your site for phones and desktops. You approve the design before we build.

  4. Build

    We develop the site with mobile-responsive layouts and a basic SEO setup, then review it with you.

  5. Launch

    We test the finished website, publish it after your approval, and verify that it works as agreed.

  6. Support

    Business Website projects include 30 days of post-launch support, with direct access to the team that built your site.

Timelines depend on the scope of your project and on how ready your content is. Your timeline is agreed in your proposal before work begins.

FAQ

Website questions

What affects the cost of a website?

The main factors are the number of pages, the features you need, whether the site is in one or two languages, and how much branding and content work is involved. Our Business Website package starts from $3,499 USD for a 5-page web presence. Prices are indicative, and every project is scoped and confirmed in a written proposal.

How long does a website take?

Timelines depend on the scope of your project and on how ready your content is. Your timeline is agreed in your proposal before work begins.

Can I update the website myself?

The Business Website package includes CMS integration. Which content-management setup suits your site is discussed during scoping and confirmed in your proposal.

Can you build an Arabic and English website?

Arabic and English websites, including Arabic right-to-left (RTL) layouts, can be discussed during scoping. The language setup — which pages are in each language and who provides the Arabic content — is confirmed in your proposal before work begins.

What happens after launch?

Business Website projects include 30 days of post-launch support, and you keep direct access to the team that built your site.

Do you work with businesses outside Qatar?

Yes. Most of our website projects are for businesses in Qatar, and we also build websites remotely for clients across the GCC and further afield, including the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman, India, the United Kingdom, the United States and Europe, with project management, design reviews and development handled online.
