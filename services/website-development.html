How we build it

From first call to launch

A clear process, so you know what happens at each step and what we need from you.

  1. Discovery

    We discuss your goals, your customers and the pages your site needs. You get a written proposal with scope, timeline and pricing.

  2. Structure and content

    We agree the page structure and what content is needed — text, images and your logo or brand assets — and who provides each part.

  3. Design

    We design the look and layout of your site for phones and desktops. You approve the design before we build.

  4. Build

    We develop the site with mobile-responsive layouts and a basic SEO setup, then review it with you.

  5. Launch

    We test the finished site, publish it and check that everything works as agreed.

  6. Support

    Starter projects include 30 days of post-launch support, with direct access to the team that built your site.

Timelines depend on scope and on how ready your content is. As a guide, a 5-page web presence typically takes 2–3 weeks; your timeline is agreed in your proposal before work begins.

Our own site as the example

The standards we hold our own site to

The website you are reading was designed and built by AFRA DIGITAL. You can check each of these points on it yourself.

  • ✓Security headers, including a strict Content Security Policy, on every page.
  • ✓Analytics tools only run after a visitor agrees, and the choice can be changed at any time from “Cookie settings”.
  • ✓An enquiry form with labelled fields and clear error messages, which confirms with a reference number only after your message has actually been delivered.
  • ✓Layouts checked on screens from small phones to wide desktops, with keyboard navigation and a skip link.

FAQ

Website questions

What affects the cost of a website?

The main factors are the number of pages, the features you need, whether the site is in one or two languages, and how much branding and content work is involved. Our Starter package starts at QAR 5,900 for a 5-page web presence. Prices are indicative, and every project is scoped and confirmed in a written proposal.

How long does a website take?

Timelines depend on scope and on how ready your content is. As a guide, a 5-page web presence typically takes 2–3 weeks. Your timeline is agreed in your proposal before work begins.

Can I update the website myself?

The Starter package includes CMS integration. Which content-management setup suits your site is discussed during scoping and confirmed in your proposal.

Can you build an Arabic and English website?

Yes. We can design and build websites with full Arabic right-to-left (RTL) support, using Arabic-friendly typography and layouts planned for bidirectional content from the start.

What happens after launch?

Starter projects include 30 days of post-launch support, and you keep direct access to the team that built your site.

Do you work with businesses outside Qatar?

Yes. We're based in Doha and work remotely with businesses across the GCC, including the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Bahrain and Oman, with project management, design reviews and development handled online.

How do we get started?

Send us a message using the form on this page, or contact us on WhatsApp. We'll arrange a short discovery call to understand your goals, then send a proposal with scope, timeline and pricing. There's no commitment until you approve the proposal.
